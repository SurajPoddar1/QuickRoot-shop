Quick answers

Most questions are answered below. If yours isn't, message us above.

How do I check where my order is?

Open the Track order page and enter your order ID. If you can't find the ID, send us your name and phone number and we'll look it up.

When is delivery free?

Delivery is free on orders above ₹500. Below that, the delivery charge is shown in your cart before you pay.

My fruit wasn't fresh. What should I do?

Choose "Fruit quality" in the form above and send a clear photo on WhatsApp along with your order ID. We'll check it and get back to you.

Can I order in bulk or for a gift?

Yes. Choose "Bulk or gift order" and tell us what you want, how much, and the date you need it. We'll confirm availability and price.

Can I come to the shop instead?

Yes. We're at R. N. Sah Chowk, Purnea. Use the map above for directions, and check the opening hours before you come.
